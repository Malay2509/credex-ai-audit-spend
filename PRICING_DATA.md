## Notes

- All prices are in USD.
- Annual billing may offer discounts (typically 16–20%) not reflected in these monthly figures.
- Enterprise pricing is always custom — audit engine flags Enterprise use for small teams as a signal to renegotiate.
- Pricing should be re-verified quarterly as vendors adjust tiers frequently.
