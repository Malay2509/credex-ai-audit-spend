# Unit Economics & Financial Modeling

## Estimated Lead Value (LTV)
- **Service Model**: We charge a 20% contingency fee on the first year's savings, or a flat $1,500 implementation fee for startups wanting us to execute the optimizations.
- **Average Savings Found**: $6,000/year.
- **Average Deal Value**: $1,200 (if contingency) or $1,500 (if flat rate). Let's assume an average ACV (Annual Contract Value) of **$1,350**.

## CAC Assumptions (Customer Acquisition Cost)
Since our GTM motion relies heavily on product-led growth (the free audit tool) and organic community distribution, our initial CAC will be low.
- **Paid Ads (Later stage)**: Estimated CPC of $2.50 on LinkedIn/Twitter. 
- **Cost per Lead**: If 5% of paid traffic converts to a lead, Cost Per Lead (CPL) = $50.
- **Conversion to Customer**: If 10% of leads close, CAC = $500.

## Conversion Funnel Math
1. **Top of Funnel (Landing Page Visits)**: 10,000 / month
2. **Audit Started**: 40% (4,000)
3. **Audit Completed**: 80% of starts (3,200)
4. **Lead Captured (Email Submission)**: 15% of completions (480 leads)
5. **Sales Call Booked**: 10% of leads (48 meetings)
6. **Deal Closed**: 25% of meetings (12 new clients)

*Yield*: 12 clients / month. 

## Profitability Assumptions
- **Revenue**: 12 clients * $1,350 = $16,200 / month.
- **Variable Costs**: API costs (OpenAI) + hosting (Vercel, Supabase, Resend) ≈ $500 / month.
- **Gross Margin**: ~96%.
- **Net Profit**: Highly dependent on founder salaries/time spent executing the implementation. If implementation takes 5 hours per client, that's 60 hours/month of labor.

## Rough ARR Modeling (Year 1)
- **Q1**: 5 clients/mo = $6,750 MRR equivalent (project-based).
- **Q2**: 15 clients/mo = $20,250 MRR equivalent.
- **Q3**: 30 clients/mo = $40,500 MRR equivalent.
- **Q4**: 50 clients/mo = $67,500 MRR equivalent.
- **End of Year 1 Projected "ARR" (Run Rate)**: ~$800,000.
