# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

## Exact Target Users
- **Seed to Series B Startup Founders** (especially technical founders & CTOs).
- **Fractional CFOs** operating in the startup ecosystem.
- Startups that have recently raised money and are actively scrutinizing their burn rate before the next fundraising round.

## Acquisition Strategy
Our strategy is "Value First". We let users run a free audit (the core feature of this MVP) and immediately show them *real* dollar savings. 
Once they see the number (e.g., "$4,500/yr saved"), they are strongly incentivized to enter their email to keep the report or book a consultation for implementation.

### Realistic First 100 Users Strategy
1. **Direct Outreach to Startup Hubs**: Post the free audit tool in highly specific Slack/Discord communities (e.g., Y Combinator alumni, local tech hub channels, tech founder communities).
2. **Cold Email with Value**: Scrape recent funding announcements (Crunchbase) and email founders with: "You probably just gave 10% of your new seed round to SaaS vendors. Run this 2-minute audit to see how much we can claw back."
3. **Product Hunt Launch**: Package the tool as a free "AI Spend Calculator for Startups" rather than a consulting service. The goal is to get 500+ organic runs, capturing ~50 qualified leads.

### Specific Online Communities
- **Twitter/X (Tech Twitter)**: Building in public, sharing anonymized "horror stories" of startups wasting $10k/yr on unused GitHub Copilot seats.
- **Hacker News**: Post under "Show HN: I built a tool that tells you exactly how much you're overpaying for AI."
- **Indie Hackers**: Target bootstrapped founders who are highly sensitive to fixed costs.

## Unfair Distribution Channel
**Venture Capital / Accelerator Partnerships.**
We partner with early-stage VC firms. VCs want their portfolio companies to extend their runway. We provide our audit tool to their portfolios for free; in exchange, the VC firm introduces us as a trusted optimization partner. This generates highly qualified, high-intent leads with zero CAC.

## Measurable Traction Expectations (First 30 Days)
- **Traffic**: 1,000 unique visitors to the landing page.
- **Audits Completed**: 300 (30% conversion from visit to completion).
- **Leads Captured**: 60 (20% conversion from completion to email capture).
- **Consultations Booked**: 6 (10% of leads).
- **Closed Deals (Optimization Projects)**: 2.
